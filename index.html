FAQ

Common questions before you start.

Can I combine services into a custom package?+

Yes. Social media, graphics, video, websites, SEO, LinkedIn, paid campaigns and lead generation can be combined based on your business needs.

Can the package prices be changed later?+

Yes. The website keeps package and individual-service pricing in one configuration file, so rates can be updated without redesigning the site.

How do I confirm a package?+

Choose a package or service and complete the contact form. Your details will open in WhatsApp as a prepared message so the project can be confirmed directly.

Do you provide website, domain and hosting together?+

The current Business Web Launch package includes website design plus domain and hosting setup. Exact domain and hosting terms should be confirmed before payment.

Can I order only a reel, poster, article or logo?+

Yes. Individual content and design services are listed separately in the pricing section.
