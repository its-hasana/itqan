FAQ

Common questions before you start.

If your question is not listed, message us directly on WhatsApp.

How do I confirm a package?+

Choose a package, complete the enquiry form and send the prepared WhatsApp message. We will confirm scope, timeline and payment details with you directly.

Can I change the package items?+

Yes. Ready-made packages are the easiest starting point, but a custom combination can be prepared when your requirements are different.

Is ad spend included in campaign management?+

No. Paid campaign management and the advertising budget are separate unless a written quotation specifically states otherwise.

Can I order only one reel, poster or article?+

Yes. Individual content services can be ordered separately at the listed rates, subject to the final brief and scope.

How are payments handled?+

Payment instructions are confirmed directly after the scope is agreed. The listed bKash number is the same as our WhatsApp number: 01776165161.
